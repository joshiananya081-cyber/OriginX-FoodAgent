Refund guidance

Received a missing, damaged or incorrect item? Report the problem promptly and keep your order details ready.

  • Keep your order ID available.
  • Clearly describe the issue.
  • Check the actual platform's refund terms.
✕ POLICY 02

Cancellation guidance

Cancellation eligibility may depend on order status and whether food preparation has started.

  • Check your order status first.
  • Review the applicable cancellation terms.
  • Don't assume a refund is guaranteed.
